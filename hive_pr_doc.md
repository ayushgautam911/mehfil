## Hive Launches Invite-Only Platform That Gives Underground Music Collectives Full Control Over Their Events, Their Crowd, and Their Growth

*Your community already exists. Now it has infrastructure.*

---

### The Problem

You run a collective. You've spent years building a community around a sound — the right DJs, the right crowd, the right energy. And you're managing all of it through Instagram DMs, Google Forms, Venmo requests, and a WhatsApp group that's one wrong invite away from blowing up.

You don't know who's actually showing up until they're at the door. You can't control who shares the flyer. You have no data on the community you've built — just a follower count that means nothing and a DM inbox you can't keep up with.

Meanwhile, your members are missing your best events because the information is scattered across five apps and three group chats, and the one person who always knows what's happening forgot to forward the message.

The underground doesn't need more exposure. It needs better infrastructure.

---

### The Solution

**Hive** is an invite-only platform built for collectives — not audiences, not brands, not the general public.

It's delivered as two purpose-built apps:

* **Hive Console** — A dedicated management tool where you publish events, control membership, vet pass requests, set invitation chains, filter your audience, and scan passes at the door. This is your operations hub.

* **Hive** (Member App) — A minimal mobile app where your members discover your events, request passes, and build their scene identity. Invite-only. No public feed. No strangers.

Both apps share a single backend. Your members never see your management tools. You never have to operate inside a consumer app.

### For the Collective Owner

*"I used to spend Sunday mornings cross-referencing a Google Form with Venmo screenshots to figure out who actually paid. Now I open Console, approve the list, and go back to sleep."*

* **Control who walks in.** Every event pass goes through you. Members request access, you review and approve. Vet them however you want — Hive handles the guest list, you handle the vibe check. No one gets in without your say-so.

* **Control how your community grows.** Set exactly how many invitations each member can send. Freeze invitations when you want to lock the doors. Grant more to your most trusted members. Every new face traces back to someone you already approved — you can see the full invitation chain.

* **Control who sees what.** Set events as collective-wide or invitation-only. Filter your audience by attendance history and genre affinity. Preview how many members qualify before you publish. Your warehouse party and your 50-person loft set don't need the same crowd — now they don't get one.

* **Control the venue.** Set when the address drops — 3 hours before, 1 hour before, whatever you want. Pass holders get a push notification with the location at reveal time. Until then, it's locked. No screenshots leaking the address to the wrong group chat.

* **Scan at the door.** Hive Console has a built-in scanner. Each issued pass has a unique QR code. Scan it, confirm entry, track attendance — all in real time. Know exactly who showed up, not just who said they would.

* **See your community, not their data.** After the event, see anonymized aggregate stats: total attendance, genre distribution, new vs. returning faces. Understand your crowd without violating anyone's privacy. No individual data exposed — ever.

* **Free for your first year.** No platform fees, no catches. We're building this for the community, not against it.

### How It Works — For Organizers

1. **Create your collective** on Hive Console — name, description, genre, profile image. You're the admin.

2. **Invite your core crew.** Generate invitation links for your existing community. They sign up on the Hive member app. Invitations from admins are auto-approved.

3. **Post an event.** Set the lineup, date, genre, venue type, and capacity. Choose who sees it — all members or invitation-only. Set your audience filters. Set when the address drops.

4. **Vet your crowd.** Pass requests come in. You review, approve, and issue passes on your terms — handle any payment or vetting outside of Hive however you already do it.

5. **Run the door.** Open Scanner mode in Console. Scan QR codes. Track attendance in real time.

6. **Grow on your terms.** See your anonymized audience data. Adjust invitation quotas. Let your community grow through trusted referral chains — not random sign-ups.

### Why Hive, Not Instagram + Venmo?

You've made the current stack work. But it doesn't scale, and it doesn't give you control.

* **Instagram** gives you reach but not membership control. Anyone can see your story. You can't filter who shows up based on whether they've actually been to your events.

* **Google Forms** gives you a sign-up list but no identity. You don't know who these people are, who invited them, or whether they've ever been to an event before.

* **Venmo/Cash App** gives you payment but no guest list. You're cross-referencing screenshots with a spreadsheet to figure out who paid.

* **WhatsApp/Telegram** gives you communication but no structure. One wrong invite and your private channel isn't private anymore.

Hive replaces all four with a single system where membership, events, passes, and communication are connected. The invitation chain means every member traces back to someone you trust. The pass system means you approve every person who walks through the door. The data stays yours, anonymized and aggregated — we don't sell it, we don't surface it to other collectives, and we never will.

### FAQ

**Is this just another event app?** No. Hive is a private community platform built for organizers first. You don't list events on a public marketplace — you run a private collective where you control everything from membership to the door.

**Will this expose underground events to the wrong people?** The opposite. Hive gives you *more* control over who sees and attends your events — not less. Invite-only membership, referral chains you set, audience filters, admin-approved passes. The crowd stays right because you decide who's in it.

**What happens to my data?** Your member list is yours. Your event data is yours. We show you anonymized, aggregated audience stats — we never expose individual member data to other collectives, advertisers, or anyone else. We will never sell user data or run ads.

**How does Hive make money?** Premium tools for collectives — advanced analytics, larger capacity events, multi-event series management — on a subscription basis. The core platform is free for your first year. No hidden fees.

**Can I keep handling payments my own way?** Yes. Hive does not process payments. You keep using Venmo, cash, Zelle, whatever works for your community. Hive handles the guest list, pass approval, and door — you handle the money however you already do.

**Can I run a collective for non-music events?** Not yet. We're starting with underground music to stay focused. But the model works for any community-driven event ecosystem — art, comedy, food, fitness — and we'll expand when the foundation is solid.
